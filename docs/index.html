    How it works

    From raw text to a clear verdict

    A transparent method you can explain to anyone. There is no black box, and every score is backed by the phrases that produced it.

    Clean

    Text is lowercased, punctuation is removed, and common filler words are set aside.

    Split

    The remaining words are broken into short overlapping phrases, three words long by default.

    Match

    Every phrase in your document is looked up in each source to find shared wording.

    Score

    Each source receives a similarity score based on shared phrases, then sources are ranked.

    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Is my text uploaded anywhere?

    No. Every check runs entirely in your browser. Your documents never leave your device, and nothing is kept after you close the page.

    How is the similarity score calculated?

    After common words are removed, both texts are split into overlapping word phrases. The score is the number of shared phrases divided by the total number of phrases across both texts, with shared phrases counted once. This measure is known as the Jaccard index.

    What score should concern me?

    A score of 25% or more usually means large passages were copied. Between 10% and 25%, review the highlighted text. Below 10% is normal for documents that cover the same topic.

    Can it detect reworded text?

    It finds copied and lightly edited passages, including text with sentences reordered or a few words added or removed. Text that has been completely rewritten in different words will score lower.

    Which file types are supported?

    Plain-text files (.txt). You can also paste text copied from Word documents, PDFs, web pages or any other source.

    Can I use it without a browser?

    Yes. The detection engine is also available as a C++ command-line application with no external dependencies. Build instructions are in the GitHub repository.
